About us

SDAfriends brings three Seventh-day Adventist online communities together in one place, so friends and visitors can find everything we share.

S.D.A Channel | Lusaka

Our Facebook page for the Seventh-day Adventist church, based in Chunga, Lusaka. Its message is to have faith and love, and to preach the gospel through books and courses. It also hosts the Adventist Channel community, with 469 members.
